# VanRakshak AI — Problem Statement

## Challenge

Human–wildlife conflict in and around forest regions can become difficult to manage when wildlife sightings, village reports, historical incidents, livestock losses, and response activities are handled through fragmented or delayed processes.

Forest authorities need timely and reliable decision-support to identify potential conflict risks, prioritize incidents, communicate safety warnings, coordinate response teams, and support affected communities.

## Problem

The existing reactive approach can lead to:

* Delayed identification of potential conflict zones
* Fragmented wildlife sighting and incident information
* Difficulty prioritizing multiple incidents
* Delayed communication of safety warnings to nearby communities
* Manual coordination of response activities
* Complex livestock-loss compensation documentation
* Limited identification of recurring and emerging conflict hotspots
* Lack of a unified decision-support workflow

## Objective

VanRakshak AI aims to provide an **Agentic AI-based Human–Wildlife Conflict Mitigation Platform for Gir** that transforms available wildlife and incident information into predictive, coordinated, and human-supervised decision support.

The system is designed to:

1. Predict potential wildlife movement and conflict risk.
2. Generate severity-based village safety alerts.
3. Prioritize and coordinate wildlife conflict incidents.
4. Assist with livestock compensation documentation.
5. Identify recurring and emerging conflict hotspots.
6. Provide forest officers with a centralized approval and monitoring interface.
7. Maintain human control over consequential decisions.

## Proposed AI Agents

The platform consists of five specialized agents:

* **Wildlife Movement Prediction Agent** — predicts potential movement and conflict risk using historical and contextual data.
* **Village Alert & Early Warning Agent** — generates concise safety warnings in English and Gujarati.
* **Forest Response Coordination Agent** — prioritizes incidents and recommends response actions and teams.
* **Livestock Compensation Assistant Agent** — assists citizens with livestock-loss documentation and preliminary claim summaries.
* **Conflict Hotspot Dashboard Agent** — analyzes historical incidents and identifies recurring or emerging risk zones.

A **Central Orchestrator** coordinates information and workflows between these agents.

## Core Workflow

**DATA → ML PREDICTION → RISK SCORING → MULTI-AGENT COLLABORATION → HUMAN REVIEW → ACTION → FEEDBACK**

## Prototype Scope

This project is a prototype demonstrating the proposed architecture and workflow using simulated/sample data.

The system does not claim live integration with Gir Forest infrastructure or real Forest Department systems. Wildlife predictions are probabilistic and intended for decision support.

Consequential actions such as official response dispatch, public intervention, or compensation decisions remain under authorized human supervision.

## Expected Outcome

VanRakshak AI aims to demonstrate how Agentic AI, Machine Learning, explainable risk scoring, and human-in-the-loop governance can support a more:

**Predictive → Coordinated → Explainable → Responsible**

approach to human–wildlife conflict mitigation.
